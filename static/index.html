Prototype Safety Notice & Operational Boundaries

1. Potential Persons, Not Confirmed Survivors:

YOLOv8 visual detections identify human forms from overhead perspectives. Detections do not establish viability, conscious status, or biological condition.

2. Simulated Geolocation Projection:

Latitude, longitude, and distance coordinates are calculated via simulated camera raycasting and must not be used as operational GPS fixes without ground calibration.

3. Advisory Decision Support:

Resource recommendations are mathematical suitability proposals. Autonomous dispatch is strictly disabled; operational deployment mandates human coordinator authorization.

4. Dataset Independence:

VisDrone (person detection) and RescueNet (semantic segmentation) are separate academic datasets. Their combination provides an integrated prototype demonstration.
