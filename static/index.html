Safety Protocol & Prototype Operational Boundaries

1
Potential Persons Only:

Visual detections generated by the YOLOv8 model represent potential persons or figures detected in aerial imagery. They do NOT confirm survivor status, biological viability, or medical condition.

2
Simulated Geolocation:

All latitude, longitude, and distance coordinates presented in this prototype are calculated from simulated camera projection geometry. They do not constitute operational GPS fixes.

3
Advisory Recommendations & Human Authorization:

Resource recommendations are decision-support heuristics designed to assist emergency coordinators. Autonomous dispatch is strictly disabled by system policy; any operational resource deployment requires explicit human authorization.

4
Dataset Independence:

The VisDrone person detector and RescueNet semantic segmentation model were trained on separate research datasets. Their combined multimodal presentation is an operational prototype demonstration rather than an aligned single ground-truth sensor.
